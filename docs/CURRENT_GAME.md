## 1. What the player does

My Chicken Army is a chicken collection, egg-farming and squad-conquest game. The player owns one of six home plots. Chickens at home produce eggs, which the player collects and sells for cash. Cash buys new chicken offers and permanent upgrades. The player chooses a fighting squad, sends it down their lane to fixed enemy camps, captures those camps, and ultimately challenges the Chicken King. Matching chickens can be fused into larger, higher-level Giants after the Fuse Machine is researched. A separate timed research tree improves farming, combat, rolling and command tools. The Shop offers optional passes, cash products and Galaxy Crates where configured and account-eligible.

The central repeatable loop is **produce eggs → collect and sell → roll/recruit or improve chickens → choose a squad → fight/capture → reinvest**. Chickens are both economic producers and combat units, so deploying strong birds can change what remains at home to lay eggs. The game retains manual army choices; new recruits may join, replace a weaker selected unit, or stay in reserve according to the server's selection rules.

## 2. Fresh player opening and tutorial

A new `starterArmy` profile starts with **three free level-1 Chickens** (internal species ID `Duck`), **$2,400**, **three base deployment slots**, one owned roll podium and no pending offer. Commander Cluck first asks the player to collect **40 egg units** across pickups, sales and rejoins, then use the green deposit pad for an instant sale. A first free lever pull guarantees a fourth level-1 Chicken offered for **$600**. The guide then leads the player to buy Army Size 4 for **$1,200**, select four chickens and send them to Cluck Camp. The tutorial records server-confirmed milestones; an egg stack contributes its actual quantity. It has a compact single-line instruction, a portrait, GUI pointers and a world Beam/marker, with no spotlight or dimmed viewport. There is no visible Skip button; old skipped saves remain compatible and Settings can resume the guide.

The server reserves $600 for the fourth recruit and $1,200 for Army Size 4. Either required purchase can spend its own reserve first. Other purchases can spend only cash above the remaining reserve. Until guide completion, selling or fusion cannot reduce the flock below three before that recruit or four afterward. Skipping or rejoining does not release the reserve. Existing player profiles keep their previous flock, balance and tutorial cohort; supported legacy `emptyStart` profiles do not receive the three-bird grant. See [the tutorial contract](team/COMMANDER_TUTORIAL.md), [upgrade rules](UPGRADE_RULES.md), [TutorialGuide](../src/shared/TutorialGuide.luau), and [PlayerSession](../src/server/PlayerSession.luau).

## 3. World and physical places

The connected Studio Edit place contains `Workspace.MyChickenArmyMap`, with environment, roads, six home plots, six three-camp lanes, an empty optional-outpost folder and a central Chicken King arena. Each plot has a deck, a chicken yard and expanding fence, an egg deposit, a Fuse Machine, eight authored numbered roll podiums and its own spawn/yard anchors. All six plots now share Plot1's authored contents and expansion support; authored geometry belongs to Studio, while code binds and changes runtime copies. The six plots keep separate entrance positions and `PlotIndex` identities. [Plot parity](team/PLOT_PARITY.md) records the authored change.

Each player's main route is the same three fixed encounters: **Cluck Camp** (three level-1 basic Chickens), **Fort Roost** (level-1 Robo-Cluck, Mushroom Chicken and two Brown Chickens; softened October 8 from level 2), then **Ironbeak HQ** (two level-2 Robo-Clucks, level-2 Mushroom Chicken and level-1 Crystal Chicken). Since October 13 the war works like Mini War: a commander can attack any camp on any commanded lane in any order (new players take their own Cluck Camp first), each held territory adds live egg value (+5/15/20% by camp, +25% King, half while contested) and at most 8 territories can be held (research raises it to 10). The Chicken King unlocks after all three camps of any one lane are beaten, while previously earned King access remains valid. See [any-lane attacks](team/ANY_LANE_ATTACKS.md). The old rank names and expanded historical camp lists remain in `Progression` for compatibility, but the builder map's active route uses these three camps and the King. Optional outposts currently return no active map roster; the configured Supply Outpost has `Enabled=false`. Do not present either as a currently playable destination. See [Config](../src/shared/Config.luau), [Progression](../src/shared/Progression.luau), [CampaignRoute](../src/shared/CampaignRoute.luau), and [the map binding](../src/server/AuthoredMap.luau).

The purchased plot-capacity ladder is **25 / 30 / 35 / 40 / 45 / 50 / 75 / 100** owned chickens across seven purchases. The yard begins as a 30×30-stud square, extends a center stem, then widens behind the stations into a T. At maximum, the design contract specifies a 30×49 stem plus an 84×45 back section. The yard and fence expand together, and roaming, egg placement and vacuum sweeps use the actual T-shaped interior. All six plots physically support the stages. The current authored deck is 96×131.853668 studs. ## 4. Chickens, collection and rolls

The persistent catalog has **44 distinct species/appearance IDs**: **40 positive-weight normal-roll entries** and **four crate-only entries** with zero normal-roll weight. An appearance has a stable internal ID, display name, rarity, weight, base egg quantity proxy (`income`), health, damage, attack interval, body size and recruit price. Many variants share a base species' stat/price band but keep distinct collection and same-variant fusion identities. Examples of internal ID versus display name are `Duck` → Chicken, `Bucket` → Robo-Cluck, `Knight` → Magma Chicken, `Dragon` → Golden Emperor and `StormDuck` → Galaxy Overlord. See [PetCatalog](../src/shared/PetCatalog.luau).

| Rarity (Luck 0 chance) | Normal-roll appearances, most common first | Crate-only appearances |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common (60%) | Chicken, Noob Chick, Brown Chicken, Rubber Ducky, Penguin Chicken, Cardboard Commando | — |
| Uncommon (22%) | Chunky Chicken, Mushroom Chicken, Cheesy Chicken, Camo Chick, Bee Chicken, Cactus Clucker, Honeycomb Chicken, Lollipop Chicken | — |
| Rare (14%) | Robo-Cluck, Zombie Chicken, Tiger Chicken, Jungle Chicken, Bonsai Bantam, Crystal Chicken, Skeleton Chicken, Pirate Chicken, Slime Squawker, Sir Clucks-a-Lot | — |
| Epic (3%) | Magma Chicken, Frost Feather, Ninja Chicken, Shadow Stalker, Storm Wing, Chicken Jockey, Toxic Chicken | Astronaut Chicken |
| Legendary (0.95%) | Golden Emperor, Diamond Defender, Solar Flare Chicken, Water Chicken, Rainbow Rider | Alien Chicken |
| Mythic (0.06%) | Glitch Chicken, Devil Chicken, Heavenly Chicken, Galaxy Overlord | Dark Matter Chicken, Cosmic Dragon |

October 7 rarity reset: rarity follows the ten strength bands, so a rarer chicken is always stronger. See [the Luck contract](team/LUCK_WINDOW.md).

The lever rolls all currently owned podiums at once, up to the eight authored/allowed stands. A fresh player owns one; seven cash purchases unlock the others. Unowned stands are hidden and non-collidable during play. Each roll creates server-owned offers and has a reveal interval (base **2.4 seconds**); buying an offer consumes that exact offer once. A player must approach their own station to roll or recruit. Normal recruit price is the catalog price doubled for each offered level above one. The server can offer a species one level below its highest owned copy, within the older rank-specific offer ceiling; every normal species is roll-eligible from the start. Affordability, collection capacity, saved state, quote and offer identity are server-checked. See [RollService](../src/server/RollService.luau), [Rules](../src/shared/Rules.luau), and [podium progression](team/EIGHT_ROLL_PODIUMS.md).

Normal roll Luck currently uses a **moving favored strength range**. Cash Luck, the highest completed rolling-luck research tier and verified paid Luck feed the same odds calculation. Equal-stat appearances share a band's probability budget. Each normal species stays possible, and raising Luck must not lower the chance of reaching a stronger threshold. The starter guarantee, verified account's disclosed guaranteed-offer sequence, fixed Galaxy Crate pool and Giant size distribution are separate from normal odds. The Research tree shows current and next Epic+ or top-band odds; the displayed percentages are per chicken in a normal roll. See [Luck contract](team/LUCK_WINDOW.md), [ChickenLuck](../src/shared/ChickenLuck.luau), and [Rules](../src/shared/Rules.luau).

The Index shows collection discovery; My Army shows owned chickens, level, rarity, stats, portraits, selection, favorites and reserve actions. A chicken's cash level can be bought in its inspector while home, one level at a time through **level 10**. Current price is `ceil(current-level recruit price × 0.75 × 2.5^(level−1))` (October 8), so each level costs 5× the previous one: a Chicken costs $1,800, $9,000, $45,000… This keeps leveling a multi-day goal instead of a two-hour runaway. Each level doubles that chicken's egg production and adds 25% of its base HP/damage to its combat stats. Cash leveling does not create or reroll Giant size. The server preserves actual paid investment, so a normal chicken sale returns **50% of recorded investment**; free birds and Robux crate birds do not invent a refundable cash purchase. Favorites, away/active defenders and tutorial-protected birds have their own transaction restrictions. See [cash chicken levels](team/CHICKEN_CASH_UPGRADES.md), [ArmyService](../src/server/ArmyService.luau), and [Rules](../src/shared/Rules.luau).

## 5. Eggs, selling and offline income

Home chickens produce egg **quantity** based on their catalog rate, level and production benefits. Newly produced eggs share one unit cash value: **$4 plus $1 per Egg Value purchase**, before relevant bonuses. Rarity/species changes quantity rather than defining its own egg unit value. A level's base production doubles per level; research and configured paid benefits affect future production. Already spawned, carried or banked eggs retain their earned value. The yard has a world-drop limit and collection uses the server's distance/ownership rules. See [egg economy](EGG_ECONOMY.md), [EggService](../src/server/EggService.luau), and [Rules](../src/shared/Rules.luau).

Players collect eggs in the yard, then step on their owned green deposit pad. **Deposits pay immediately**, with the existing Money effect for manual deposits. The Auto Collect Eggs pass, when owned and enabled, drives a physical vacuum through the yard and unloads through the same server payout path; its automatic delivery deliberately avoids the player's manual deposit particles/sound/toast. A deposit cannot partially consume a bag if the full payout exceeds wallet headroom. Saved pending balances remain compatible. The previous queue, Selling Speed purchase board and Finish Now checkout are inactive behind `EggProcessorConfig.Enabled=false`; their boards, source backups, saved levels and paid credits are retained for an explicitly requested restoration. See [instant-sales contract](team/INSTANT_EGG_SALES.md), [vacuum](VACUUM.md), and [EggProcessorConfig](../src/shared/EggProcessorConfig.luau).

Offline cash is calculated from a saved departure production checkpoint at **2% efficiency**, initially capped at **eight hours**. Research can extend the allowance to nine, ten and twelve hours. A returned player's receipt is persisted by the server before the client displays it; research completed during absence does not retroactively change the entire checkpoint rate. See [offline cash](OFFLINE_CASH.md), [OfflineCashConfig](../src/shared/OfflineCashConfig.luau), and [OfflineCash](../src/server/OfflineCash.luau).

## 6. Cash upgrades

Six upgrade tracks appear as individual connected hexes in the same full-screen tree as research. A cash purchase applies immediately: **no research job, free researcher, camp conquest, rank or King milestone is required**. The tree is a shared visual surface, not a shared prerequisite system. The physical Upgrades board is archived. The server still checks the player's current quote, funds, readiness, track maximum and actual plot/podium support. A fresh opening's protected cash reserve also applies. See [upgrade rules](UPGRADE_RULES.md), [cash tree](team/CASH_UPGRADES_IN_TREE.md), and [UpgradeService](../src/server/UpgradeService.luau).

| Track | Current effect and maximum |
| --- | --- |
| Egg Laying Speed | 25 purchases; production multiplier starts at 1× and adds 0.15 per level, reaching 4.75× before other benefits. |
| Egg Value | 25 purchases; newly produced egg unit value rises from $4 to $29. |
| Plot Capacity | Seven purchases, from 25 to 100 owned chickens, with the physical square-to-T yard/fence stages. Prices: $480, $1,200, $2,400, $2,880, $63,000, $725,760, $3,628,800. |
| Army Size | Base deployment capacity starts at three and can be bought up to ten. The Bigger Army pass adds five effective slots separately. |
| Luck | 20 purchases; each adds 0.10 Luck strength (October 7 retune, cap 3×). Research and eligible paid benefits combine with it. |
| Roll Slots | Starts at one, max eight podiums. Slot 2–8 prices: $1,200 / $4,800 / $120,000 / $6 million / $150 million / $300 million / $500 million. |

The former Selling Speed track has no current offer. Saved seller levels remain stored. The eight-podium 8–9-hour target was an initial model, not a measured promise for the combined current economy, which has since added chicken cash leveling and a larger yard.

## 7. Army commands, combat and conquest

The server owns squad selection and validates exact IDs. My Army supports manual selection, Select Best, favorites, equip/unequip and a saved selected order. Purchased base capacity tops out at ten, with a verified Bigger Army pass adding five effective slots. A selected/deployed army can include **at most one Colossal** chicken, while smaller Giants use ordinary slots. Saved lineups, Select Best, manual selection and battle replacement all respect that limit. See [ArmyService](../src/server/ArmyService.luau) and [Giant design](team/GIANT_CHICKEN_DESIGN.md).

The bottom contextual row is **Call Army · My Army · My Base** outside the player's plot. At home, the side commands collapse behind the larger center My Army icon. Call Army requires completed `CallArmy` research ($1,000, 60 base seconds); when unlocked, it transfers the selected squad to follow the player, withdrawing an eligible outgoing battle first. My Base returns the player and owned chickens to their yard. These commands have server checks and short cooldowns; the client cannot choose ownership or destinations. See [army commands](team/ARMY_COMMANDS.md), [ArmyCommandService](../src/server/ArmyCommandService.luau), and [HudController](../src/client/HudController.luau).

At an available camp, a native prompt sends the selected squad down its route. Physical chickens march, fight with individual HP/damage/attack timing, recover and rejoin under server control. The fixed main-camp defenders detect nearby arrivals and move to engage. Chicken and guard health bars appear in combat; splash attacks and NPC kill cash use shared rules. A player can own a captured camp persistently while commanding the squad elsewhere. Since October 8 the army marches home about 5 seconds after any main-route capture or King clear, so home chickens keep laying; armies cannot station on ground the player already holds. Camp prompts show the odds in ObjectText ("Very tough / Close fight / Should win · N defenders · $/kill") and locked targets show their reason on a heading billboard. A rival at a King arena someone else holds fights a full fresh King; the King boss bounty pays in full once per player (`kingBountyPaid`) and 10% on repeats. Conquest itself is a continuous **25-second** meter advanced by living, arrived chickens in the capture area during combat. Progress pauses if no eligible attackers remain, and completion still requires defenders to be down. The meter is a four-section red bar; victory shows a green owned marker, owner name and portrait on the existing authored flag. Explicit unclaim immediately restores hostile ownership/guards and starts a fresh conquest without replacing the player's chicken identities. Failed attacks retain a separate gradual enemy recovery path. Camp-route prerequisites and King access remain separate from cash upgrades and from research. See [conquest contract](team/CONQUEST_REFERENCE.md), [Battle](../src/server/Battle.luau), and [BattleService](../src/server/BattleService.luau).

The configured Supply Outpost/territory contest code exists but the site is disabled in [Config](../src/shared/Config.luau); it is not part of the currently active world route. The current fixed camp route and Chicken King are the active conquest destinations.

## 8. Fuse Machine, Giants and Colossals

Manual fusion is enabled, but a new player must finish the **Fuse Machine** Army research node: **$5,000, 120 base seconds**, prerequisite Start. Older valid earned access survives save migration. A camp capture alone no longer newly unlocks fusion. The player uses their own plot's machine, chooses an exact triple of matching **species, stars and level**, and pays `ceil(one input's normal recruit price × 25%)`. The server validates machine proximity, access, ownership, inputs, cash and quote before one atomic consume/deduct/result transaction. Auto Merge remains disabled. Favorites, away/active defenders, level-capped chickens and tutorial-protected minimum flock members cannot be consumed. Fusion remains subject to its separate rank-based merge level ceilings (3/5/7/10), unlike cash chicken levels. See [merging](MERGING.md), [ArmyService](../src/server/ArmyService.luau), and [Rules](../src/shared/Rules.luau).

Every successful triple produces one chicken of the same appearance, one level higher, with a `Giant.Chance(level)` chance (10% + 2% per input level, guaranteed after 8 fusions without one) to become a Giant (October 14; see `src/shared/GiantChicken.luau`). Its visual size is saved in studs and never rerolled on equip or rejoin. Re-fusing preserves at least the largest input size. Fresh size bands are Common 4–6, Uncommon 4.5–8, Rare 6–12, Epic 8–17, Legendary 11–22 and Mythic 15–27.5 studs, with fresh **20+ Colossal** chances only for Legendary (1%) and Mythic (3%). The 20+ tail favors its smaller sizes. Existing large low-rarity Giants keep earned sizes. Size is visual and does not add another income or combat multiplier beyond the normal next-level reward. The rig grows visually while keeping an ordinary walking collision/root size. A successful UI action closes the Fuse popup so the physical garage can reveal and walk out the actual owned result chicken. See [Giant design](team/GIANT_CHICKEN_DESIGN.md), [GiantChicken](../src/shared/GiantChicken.luau), and [MergeStand](../src/server/MergeStand.luau).

Players may own multiple Colossals but select/deploy only one. The inventory offers **Replace Colossal**. If the old one is deployed, its replacement waits until it has returned. The 20-stud rule uses the saved size, not the appearance rarity.

## 9. Research and Operations

The full-screen black hex tree has **Start plus 62 paid research nodes**. Research is permanent, paid with earned cash and completed after a server-quoted timer. It starts with one researcher; Command nodes can raise this to two and then three simultaneous jobs. Prerequisite completion, revision, cost, available researcher and duration are server-validated. Cancellation refunds the actual debit once, and absolute finish times continue while offline. Higher completed bonuses within one family replace lower tiers rather than stacking every tier. The same screen interleaves the six *instant cash upgrade* branches, but those branches have no research prerequisite. The current tree uses adjacent compact branches around Start; U, R and the HUD Upgrades entry open it. See [research](team/RESEARCH.md), [ResearchCatalog](../src/shared/ResearchCatalog.luau), [ResearchRules](../src/shared/ResearchRules.luau), and [ResearchService](../src/server/ResearchService.luau).

| Research branch | What it can unlock or improve |
| --- | --- |
| Army | Damage, maximum health, march speed, armor, recovery, Rally, boss damage, Call Army and the Fuse Machine. Rally gives +20% damage for 8 seconds, later 12, with a 90-second cooldown. |
| Economy | Future egg quantity/value, manual pickup radius, NPC bounty and offline allowance. |
| Command | Research speed, researcher count, conquest speed, player movement, one to three saved named formations and three Operation tiers. |
| Rolling | Six Luck-strength tiers and six podium roll-speed tiers. These affect normal roll distribution/reveal timing, not the Galaxy Crate or Giant size odds. |

**Operations** are nine separate finite army encounters: Veteran Patrol, Iron Roost, Veteran Command; Shadow Battery, Last Line, Elite Commander; Royal Vanguard, Crown's Shield and Royal Chicken King. Each tier is unlocked through Command research. They use fixed authored rosters and a separate cloned King arena, without wallet/power scaling, reinforcements or squad respawns during the mission. Defeat the enemy and complete its capture phase for a first-clear cash reward ($2.5M to $2.5B by tier since October 8); replays pay 10%. The Royal Chicken King boss has 80,000 HP and 900 splash damage so a maxed free 10-slot army can win. Abandonment, death or leaving ends a mission without completion payout. Three wins in a set earn that tier's cosmetic banner; the Royal set also shows Royal Commander at home. See [OperationCatalog](../src/shared/OperationCatalog.luau), [OperationService](../src/server/OperationService.luau), and [research contract](team/RESEARCH.md).

## 10. Shop and paid features

The Shop catalog lists configured passes for Auto Collect Eggs, 2x Cash, 2x Egg Spawn, Bigger Army, 2x Luck, Egg Magnet, Commander Banner and Double NPC Bounty. **Auto Merge is excluded from the live shop because its feature switch is false.** Pass benefits are checked against verified entitlement and account eligibility; a displayed catalog entry is not proof of a completed purchase. Native Roblox checkout and server receipt/delivery remain authoritative. The catalog also defines four cash developer products and one-, three- and ten-pack Galaxy Crate products. Prices labeled `proposedPrice` in source are recorded design values; the client reads the buyer's actual current Marketplace price. See [ShopCatalog](../src/shared/ShopCatalog.luau), [CommerceService](../src/server/CommerceService.luau), and [ProductService](../src/server/ProductService.luau).

Galaxy Crates use durable paid credits and a fixed server pool when the account is eligible to open them: Astronaut Chicken **40%**, Alien Chicken **30%**, Galaxy Overlord **20%**, Dark Matter Chicken **9%**, Cosmic Dragon **1%**. Since October 8 Cosmic Dragon (+35%) and Dark Matter (+15%) out-damage a free Galaxy Overlord. A credit is consumed together with the awarded chicken, and full collection capacity leaves the credit intact. This pool is separate from normal-roll Luck and species weights. The configured restricted-account treatment for paid random mechanics uses disclosed deterministic guaranteed normal-roll offers instead of silently consuming a crate. See [CrateService](../src/server/CrateService.luau) and [ShopCatalog](../src/shared/ShopCatalog.luau). Finish Now is preserved only as a disabled archived product.

## 12. Saving, authority and code layout

`GameServer` starts world, egg, roll, upgrade, battle, army command, research, Operation, commerce, product, tutorial, leaderboard and telemetry services. Clients send `RequestAction`; the server returns `StateChanged` and broadcasts `BattleChanged`. The server limits request rate, rejects unloaded/closing/save-lost sessions and checks ownership, location, quotes and saved revisions at each transaction boundary. It is authoritative for cash, eggs, offers, owned birds, selection, combat, claims, research, purchase delivery and persistence. Clients render snapshots and effects. See [GameServer](../src/server/GameServer.luau), [PlayerSession](../src/server/PlayerSession.luau), and [CODE_MAP.md](CODE_MAP.md).

Profiles use the `DuckArmy_Pond_v2` store name and current data revision 11. The saved state includes wallet, chickens and their stable IDs/species/level/investment/optional Giant size, selected army, favorites, upgrade levels and owned podiums, carried/legacy egg value, camp/King progress, other-lane camp progress (`laneCamps`), tutorial cohort/milestones, research completed IDs/jobs/formations/Operation wins, offline checkpoint, commerce credits/receipts and preserved seller state. Loading validates rather than silently accepting malformed research/Giant data. Save leases, retries and status guard transactions. Existing-player compatibility is a deliberate requirement: older plot levels migrate once without losing effective yard, missing old roll-slot fields keep their historical ownership, legacy fusion access survives, and disabled seller balances/credits remain. See [DataService](../src/server/DataService.luau), [PlayerSession](../src/server/PlayerSession.luau), and feature contracts above.

Rojo maps `src/server` to `ServerStorage.PetRoll`, `src/client` to `StarterPlayer.StarterPlayerScripts.PetRoll`, `src/shared` to `ReplicatedStorage.PetRoll.Shared`, `src/animations` to `ReplicatedStorage.PetRoll.ChickenClips`, and the two bootstraps to their existing Script/LocalScript destinations. The map, rigs, GUI template, audio and most imported art remain Studio-owned. `tests/` is optional and outside the default sync; `archive/` contains retained sources, not active gameplay. A scripts-only Rojo build is not a playable place. Read [README.md](../README.md), [CODE_MAP.md](CODE_MAP.md), [EDITING.md](EDITING.md), and [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) before implementation work.
