## Unsure: the new Operations HUD shortcut

Before this change, the Operations page could only be opened by clicking a **completed** S15/S16/S17 node in the research tree (`ResearchBrowser:Activate`). A player who beat the King but has no S15 would have Veteran unlocked with no way to reach it. I added a small "Operations" HUD button in `ResearchExtras` (`OperationsShortcut`, navigation colour). It sits in Rally's slot (right side, y=.58). Rally only shows while Fighting, and this button only shows while the army is Home or Following, with tier ≥ 1, no active Operation, no panel, research tree or dialogue open. **Please check its placement in a playtest.** If you'd rather have a different entry point (for example an Operations button in the Army inspector next to Formations in `ChickenBrowser.luau`, or making the S15 node open Operations when `state.operations.tier >= 1`), that change would be in files I don't own. To drop the shortcut, delete the three `self.operations` lines in `Extras.new`, the visibility block in `UpdateRally` and the `:Destroy()` call.
